Your information will be handled in accordance with Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).     YOUR PERSONAL REFLECTION GUIDE

    Make your money work for what matters.

    1. What do you want more room for?

    Pick one value from your Top 5. What would it look like in your everyday life if this value had 10% more room?

    2. Where do you feel supported — or stretched?

    Which of your answers feels most true? What is one real-life example from the past month?

    3. Choose one small next step.

    Finish this sentence: “To make more room for what matters, one thing I could clarify or change is…”

    Keep it small. You don't have to solve your whole financial life today.

    SAINTHAPPY MONEY MAPPING

    Let's connect the dots.

    Your values are the starting point. A Money Mapping conversation looks at how your financial system supports the life you want to build — without assuming you need a particular product or that everything needs fixing.

    What we can explore together

    Cash flow & breathing room
    What your money needs to cover now, and what can safely be set aside.

    Liquidity & resilience
    How much flexibility you have if life changes or an unexpected cost comes up.

    Protection & risk
    What could disrupt your plans, and where a gap may or may not exist.

    Capital & future choices
    How your resources can support the priorities and options you care about.
